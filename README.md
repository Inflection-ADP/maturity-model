## The "Why"
We’ve spent years perfecting Internal Developer Platforms (IDP) to help humans move faster. But the world is shifting. We are moving toward **Agentic Developer Platforms (ADP)**—ecosystems where AI agents, not just people, navigate infrastructure to deliver value.

Agents require a different level of rigor. They need consistent contracts, observable systems, and predictable automation. **Inflection** is an open-access framework built to help you measure exactly where your organization stands on this journey and provide a prioritized roadmap to get you "Agent-Ready."

---

## The Seven Dimensions of Maturity
We evaluate platform health across seven critical pillars, ranking them from **Manual & Undocumented** to **Autonomous & Agentic**.

| Dimension | Why it matters for Agents |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Documentation & Context** | Agents need a "knowledge base" to understand the *intent* behind the infra. |
| **API & Tooling Consistency** | Predictable interfaces are the only way agents can interact without breaking things. |
| **CI/CD Automation** | The execution engine. If it isn't automated, an agent can't trigger it. |
| **Observability** | The feedback loop. Agents must "see" the result of their actions in real-time. |
| **Safety** | Guardrails that prevent autonomous actions from scaling a mistake. |
| **Security & Zero Trust** | Essential for autonomous systems operating in high-governance environments. |
| **FinOps** | Ensuring that agentic automation doesn't lead to unchecked infrastructure spend. |

---

## How it Works
Inflection is designed to be high-impact and low-friction.
*   **7-Minute Assessment:** A diagnostic tool to benchmark your current state.
*   **Peer Comparison:** See how your maturity stacks up against industry averages (anonymized).
*   **Prioritized Roadmap:** Get a clear "To-Do" list based on the gaps found in your dimensions.
