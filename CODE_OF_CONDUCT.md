# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Treat everyone in this project the way you would want to be treated by someone you respect. Be direct. Be honest. Be constructive. Do not make it personal.

---

## What this project is

Inflection is a technical project about platform engineering. People who contribute come from different backgrounds, companies, and levels of experience. Some will agree with the framework. Some will challenge it. Both are welcome.

Good technical disagreement — where someone explains clearly why they think something is wrong and what they think is better — makes the project stronger. That kind of pushback is not just tolerated here, it is encouraged.

---

## What is not welcome

The line is between disagreeing with ideas and attacking people.

These behaviors are not acceptable:

- Personal insults or dismissive language directed at a person rather than their argument
- Harassment of any kind, in public threads or private messages
- Deliberate intimidation or attempts to silence someone
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission

If you disagree with a contribution or a decision, explain why. If you think something is technically wrong, show your reasoning. That is how this project improves.

---

## If something goes wrong

If you experience or witness behavior that crosses the line above, email [saisravan@gmail.com](mailto:saisravan@gmail.com). Describe what happened and include any relevant links or context.

Every report will be read and taken seriously. Depending on what happened, the response might be a private conversation, a warning, or removal from the project.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where this project is discussed under the Inflection-ADP organization.

---

## In plain terms

Work hard, be honest, argue about ideas not people, and help make the project better than you found it.
